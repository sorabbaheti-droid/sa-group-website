SA Group is two foundries under one roof of standards: Standard Alloys India and Surge Alloys, both in Ajmer, Rajasthan.

We started as a fabrication shop in 1991 and lit our first induction furnace in 1994. Today three plants cover 40,000 square metres and pour about 14,000 tonnes a year.

We make the parts that wear out first: crusher liners, mill and VRM parts, kiln and cooler castings, and the spares a plant cannot wait months for.

Every heat is tested in our own laboratory, and every casting can be traced back to it. See how a part is made.

Over 500 plants and OEMs buy from us, from FLSmidth and Metso to UltraTech and National Cement Kenya.

How a part is made

Design

Your drawing, sample or sketch becomes a method, checked in simulation before any metal is poured.

Pour

Induction crucibles up to 6 tonnes, up to 10 tonnes of liquid metal per pour, into no-bake Alphaset moulds.

Quench

Furnaces up to 12 tonnes with water and oil quench. Manganese steel reaches the water in about 45 seconds.

Machine

VTLs, VMCs, a plano miller and a 6.5 m special-purpose machine finish the part to your drawing.

Inspect

Dimensions, DPT, MPI and UT, assembly fit-up and paint thickness, checked before every dispatch.

What buyers ask us first

What do you need to quote?

A drawing (PDF, DWG, DXF or STEP), the grade or the duty, quantity and yearly volume, and whether you need it machined. No drawing? Send the old part or photos with key dimensions and we reverse-engineer it.

What sizes can you make?

Single pieces from 3 kg to 7,000 kg, with machining up to 6,500 mm diameter.

Which grades do you pour?

Manganese steel, high chrome iron, Ni-Hard, Cr-Mo steel, heat-resistant stainless (HRCS, HH, HK), carbon and low alloy steel, to IS, ASTM or your own specification.

Do you supply outside India?

Yes. Castings ship from Ajmer to customers on four continents, packed and documented for export.

What reports come with the castings?

Chemistry, hardness, UTS and impact, microstructure, dimensional and NDT reports. Every casting is traceable to its heat.

How do I know when it ships?

The date is confirmed with the order, and you get production updates until dispatch, with final QC clearance before the truck leaves.
