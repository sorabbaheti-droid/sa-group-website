Before you ask

What do you need to quote?

A drawing (PDF, DWG, DXF or STEP), the grade or the duty, quantity and yearly volume, and whether you need it machined. No drawing? Send the old part or photos with key dimensions and we reverse-engineer it.

Which grades do you pour?

Manganese steel, high chrome iron, Ni-Hard, Cr-Mo steel, heat-resistant stainless (HRCS, HH, HK), carbon and low alloy steel, to IS, ASTM or your own specification.

Do you supply outside India?

Yes. Castings ship from Ajmer to customers on four continents, packed and documented for export.

What reports come with the castings?

Chemistry, hardness, UTS and impact, microstructure, dimensional and NDT reports. Every casting is traceable to its heat.

Are you certified?

ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015 and ISO 45001:2018, with SBTi emission targets and 630 kW of rooftop solar.

How do I know when it ships?

The date is confirmed with the order, and you get production updates until dispatch, with final QC clearance before the truck leaves.
