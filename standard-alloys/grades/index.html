Materials and grades

Six alloy families, poured to IS, ASTM or your own specification, and chosen for the duty, not the catalogue.

The right grade is decided by what the part meets in service: impact, sliding abrasion, heat or all three. Our metallurgists confirm the grade with every quotation, and every heat is tested before the casting ships.

Seven mistakes when buying wear castings

And what to ask your foundry instead.

  • Grade picked by habit, not by dutyMatch the alloy to the feed, impact and temperature the part will meet.
  • Manganese steel quenched too slowlyWater quench fast from the furnace, or the steel embrittles and cracks.
  • No method design before pouringSimulate the casting method to keep shrinkage out of critical sections.
  • Wrong alloy for the hot zoneVerify Cr-Ni chemistry on a spectrometer before the part ships.
  • Raw tolerances acceptedProof-machine and check fit-up so the part installs first time.
  • No record of the heatInsist on chemistry, hardness and NDT reports traceable to the heat.
  • Waiting on overseas lead timesWork with a foundry that already holds your patterns.
