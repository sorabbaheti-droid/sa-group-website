Table liners

The table liner is the track the rollers run on. It wears a groove under the rollers, and the depth of that groove is often what forces the mill to stop.

Works in
Grinding table segments of vertical roller mills, and bull ring segments of bowl mills.
Industries
Cement, Thermal power and steel
Materials
High chrome iron, Ni-Hard
Size
Single pieces from 3 kg to 7 tonnes, to drawing
Supplied

What we control

  • Hardened through the sectionWhite iron alloyed and heat treated for the thickness of each segment, not just its surface.
  • Structure checkedMicrostructure examined with an image analyser, so carbide and matrix are verified, not assumed.
  • Fit as a setSegments checked against each other and against gauges so they assemble without forcing.
  • Machined to drawingBores, journals and fixing faces finished on vertical lathes and machining centres.
