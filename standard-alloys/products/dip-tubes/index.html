Dip tubes

The dip tube stops raw meal short-cutting the cyclone. When segments distort or drop, separation falls and more meal circulates to the upper stages. Fuel and fan power rise with it.

Works in
Segmented dip tubes, also called vortex finders or thimbles, in preheater cyclones.
