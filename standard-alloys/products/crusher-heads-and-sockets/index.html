What we control

  • Profile to drawingPatterns made from the drawing or from a worn sample, and kept on file for repeat orders.
  • Fast quenchManganese parts go from furnace to water in about 45 seconds for full toughness.
  • Machined seatsSeating faces and bores turned on vertical lathes, then checked with gauges.
  • Surface checkedDye penetrant on manganese steel; magnetic particle and ultrasonic testing on structural steel.
