Clamps

Clamps hold roller and table segments in place under very high grinding load. A clamp that cracks or loosens can free a segment inside a running mill.

What we control

  • Hardened through the sectionWhite iron alloyed and heat treated for the thickness of each segment, not just its surface.
  • Structure checkedMicrostructure examined with an image analyser, so carbide and matrix are verified, not assumed.
  • Fit as a setSegments checked against each other and against gauges so they assemble without forcing.
