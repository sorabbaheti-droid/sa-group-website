Blow bars

Blow bars strike the feed at rotor speed and throw it against the impact plates. No single alloy suits every rock: the choice is a trade between wear life and the risk of breakage.

03

What we control

  • Grade set by the dutyFeed size, abrasiveness and the risk of tramp metal decide the alloy, not habit.
  • Fast quenchManganese parts go from furnace to water in about 45 seconds, so carbides stay in solution.
  • Tested every heatChemistry by spectrometer and hardness on every heat; tensile and impact tests as specified.
  • Fit where it mattersPin bores, seats and clamping faces machined to drawing.
