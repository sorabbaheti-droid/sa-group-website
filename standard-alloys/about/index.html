Since 1991

A fabrication shop that became a specialist foundry for the parts that take the hardest wear and heat.

Standard Alloys India began in 1991 as a fabrication shop in Ajmer, Rajasthan, and lit its first induction furnace in 1994. Over three decades we have built a foundry around one idea: a casting should be specified for the duty it will meet. Today our three facilities have about 14,000 tonnes a year of installed capacity, pouring single pieces from 3 kg to 7 tonnes for more than 500 industrial customers, in plants anywhere in the world.

How we work

  • Duty firstWe ask where a part runs and how it fails before we recommend a grade.
  • In-house controlMethod, pattern, melt, heat treatment, machining and testing in our own plant.
  • Evidence with every castingChemistry, hardness and NDT reports, traceable to the heat.
