Since 1991

A fabrication shop in Ajmer that became a specialist foundry for the parts that wear out first.

Standard Alloys India began in 1991 as a fabrication shop in Ajmer, Rajasthan, and lit its first induction furnace in 1994. Over three decades we have built a foundry around one idea: a casting should be specified for the duty it will meet. Today we cast single pieces from 3 kg to 7 tonnes and ship them to plants anywhere in the world.
