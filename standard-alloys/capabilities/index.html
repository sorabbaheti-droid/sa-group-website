01

Engineering and method

Every new part starts with a method: gating, risering and feeding designed in-house, with casting simulation through a specialist partner for critical parts. Worn parts and samples are reverse-engineered when no drawing exists.

  • Methoding and casting design
  • CAD and profile design
  • Reverse engineering from samples
  • Simulation with a specialist partner
02

Pattern shop

Patterns are made in-house in wood, polystyrene and aluminium, cut on a CNC router. More than 10,000 patterns are already on file, so repeat orders go straight to moulding.

  • 10,000+ patterns on file
  • Wood, polystyrene and aluminium
  • CNC router pattern making
03

Melting

Induction melting in crucibles from 500 kg to 6 tonnes. A dual-track power supply brings up to 10 tonnes of liquid metal to a single pour, so heavy pieces are cast in one go.

  • Crucibles of 500, 1,500, 3,000 and 6,000 kg
  • Up to 10 t liquid metal per pour
  • Spectrometer analysis on every heat
04

Moulding

Alphaset no-bake moulding gives strong, dimensionally stable moulds for pieces from 3 kg to 7,000 kg. Sand is mechanically reclaimed and reused.

  • Alphaset mixers of 8 and 20 t/h
  • Mould pre-heater and shell core shooter
  • Sand reclamation of 6 and 12 t/h
05

Heat treatment

Gas-fired box furnaces with water quench, and bogie-hearth furnaces with air and oil quench. Manganese steel goes from furnace to water in about 45 seconds.

  • Two 10 t box furnaces, water quench
  • Two 12 t bogie-hearth furnaces, air and oil quench
  • Solution treatment, hardening and tempering
06

Fettling and machining

Castings are fettled, proof-machined and finished to drawing on CNC and conventional vertical lathes, machining centres and a 6,500 mm special-purpose machine.

  • Two CNC vertical lathes and a 1,350 mm VTL
  • Machining centres to 1,600 × 800 mm
  • Plano miller, lathes and radial drills
