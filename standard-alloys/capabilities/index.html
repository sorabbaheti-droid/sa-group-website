Everything under one roof

From the method to the machined part, nothing that decides the life of a casting leaves our control.

01

Engineering and method

Every new part starts with a method: gating, risering and feeding simulated before a pattern is made. Worn parts and samples can be reverse-engineered when no drawing exists.

  • Casting simulation
  • Reverse engineering from samples
  • Drawing review and design for casting
02

Pattern shop

Patterns are made in-house in wood, polystyrene and metal. More than 10,000 patterns are already on file, so repeat orders go straight to moulding.

  • 10,000+ patterns on file
  • Wood, polystyrene and metal patterns
  • Pattern maintenance and modification
03

Melting

Induction melting in crucibles of up to 6 tonnes, with up to 10 tonnes of liquid metal in a single pour, so heavy single pieces are cast in one go.

  • Induction furnaces, crucibles to 6 t
  • Up to 10 t liquid metal per pour
  • Spectrometer check before tapping
04

Moulding

No-bake Alphaset moulding gives strong, dimensionally stable moulds for pieces from 3 kg to 7,000 kg.

  • Alphaset no-bake process
  • Single pieces 3 kg to 7,000 kg
  • Sand reclamation
05

Heat treatment

Box and bogie-hearth furnaces up to 12 tonnes, with water and oil quench. Manganese steel goes from furnace to water in about 45 seconds.

  • Furnaces to 12 t
  • Water and oil quench
  • Solution annealing, hardening, tempering
06

Machining

CNC and conventional vertical turret lathes, vertical machining centres, a plano miller and a special-purpose machine finish parts to drawing.

  • Turning up to 6,500 mm diameter
  • VTL, VMC and plano milling
  • Assembly fit-up checks
