Traceable to the heat

Every heat is analysed in our own laboratory, and every casting carries its heat number from the mould to your plant.

From melt to dispatch

One heat number follows the casting through every step, so any report can be traced back to the metal it came from.

Laboratory

  • Chemical analysis on an Ametek spectrometer, for every heat
  • Optical microscope with image analyser for microstructure
  • Tensile, impact and bench hardness testing
  • Sand laboratory: grain size, clay and loss on ignition, compression and shear strength

Non-destructive testing

  • Ultrasonic testing of critical sections
  • Magnetic particle inspection
  • Dye penetrant testing

Manganese steel is non-magnetic and coarse-grained, so its surface is checked with dye penetrant, and its soundness is built in through the method.

Dimensions and fit

  • Dimensional inspection against the drawing
  • Profile and thread gauges; surface roughness
  • Assembly and segment fit checks
  • Wet and dry film thickness of the paint
  • 3D laser scanning and portable CMM through partners
