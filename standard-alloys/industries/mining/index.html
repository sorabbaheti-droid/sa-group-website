In a mine, a liner that fails early costs more in lost tonnes than the part itself. Primary crushers need tough steel that hardens under impact; mills need a balance of toughness and wear; feeders and conveyors need parts that take both. We cast for each duty and machine where the fit matters.

Stage 1

Primary crushing

Jaw and gyratory crushers take run-of-mine rock in heavy, repeated blows. Manganese steel is the standard here because its surface hardens as it is struck.
