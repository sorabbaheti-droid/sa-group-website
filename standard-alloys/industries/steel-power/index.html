Thermal power and steel

Coal crusher and mill parts for power stations and wear parts for the material-handling lines of steel plants.

  2. Coal handling
  3. Coal grinding
  4. Steel plants

A coal-fired station wears out its crushers and its mills; a steel plant wears out the crushers, chutes and spouts that handle its raw materials. Both need abrasion resistance where material slides and toughness where it strikes, specified part by part.

Stage 1

Coal handling

Hammer crushers size coal before the mills. Chutes guide it between conveyors. Parts take impact and abrasion from coal, shale and stone.
