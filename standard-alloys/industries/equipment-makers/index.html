Equipment makers

Original equipment makers need a foundry that works to their drawing, their standard and their schedule. We review the drawing, design the method, cast, heat treat and machine, and send the reports your quality team needs.

Stage 1

Drawing review and method

Our engineers review the drawing for castability, then agree the method, the grade and the inspection plan with you before a pattern is made.

Your drawingYour standard
  1. Design-for-casting feedbackHot spots, shrinkage risks and machining allowances raised before tooling.
  2. Method designGating and feeding designed in-house, with casting simulation through a specialist partner.
  3. Inspection planWhat is tested, how and to which standard, agreed before production.
Stage 2

Pattern and first article

Patterns are made in-house. The first casting is fully inspected and reported before series production starts.

First article inspection
  1. Pattern makingWood, polystyrene and aluminium patterns, cut on a CNC router.
  2. First article inspectionDimensions, chemistry, hardness and NDT, reported to you.
  3. Approval and releaseSeries production starts on your sign-off.
