Privacy notice

How we handle the information you send us through this website.

Who we are

Standard Alloys India Pvt. Ltd., F-26B & 27, Parbatpura Industrial Area, Ajmer 305002, Rajasthan, India. Questions about this notice: sales@standardalloysindia.com.

What we collect

Only what you choose to send us: your name, company, email, phone, country and the details of your enquiry or job application, with any drawings, photographs or CV you attach. We also count visits to the site in an anonymous, aggregated way.

Why we use it

To answer your enquiry, prepare a quotation, process your application and keep a record of our correspondence. We do not use it for anything else without asking you.

Drawings and technical data

Drawings, samples and specifications you send are used only to quote and make your parts. They are not shared outside the company except with partners who help us make or test that part, under confidentiality.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. We share it only with service providers who run our email and business systems, and with authorities where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

For as long as we need it to deal with your enquiry or order, and afterwards for the period that business and tax law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw a consent you gave, by writing to the address above. We handle personal data in line with applicable law, including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Changes

We may update this notice. The date below shows the latest version.
