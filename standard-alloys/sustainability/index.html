Responsible foundry

A casting that lasts longer is the first saving. We are also cutting the energy and emissions behind every tonne we pour.

A casting that lasts longer means fewer parts melted, shipped and replaced. That is where our work on grades and methods starts. In the plant, we have set absolute targets for our own emissions and put seven initiatives to work.

Emission targets

Absolute reduction targets, set on a 1.5 °C pathway using the Science Based Targets initiative method.

  • 19.4%Scope 1 reduction target
  • 11.2%Scope 2 reduction target
  • 13%Scope 1 and 2 combined

Seven initiatives in the plant

  • 01Water scrubberCleans the fume from the induction furnaces.
  • 02EconomiserRecovers waste heat from the heat-treatment furnace.
  • 03630 kW of solarRooftop solar supplying the plant.
  • 04Sand reclamationSilica sand mechanically reclaimed and reused in moulding.
  • 05Dust collectionDust collector on the sand plant.
  • 06Rainwater harvestingRainwater collected and returned to the ground.
  • 07Active melt managementMelting monitored and controlled to cut energy per tonne.

Environmental management

  • ISO 14001:2015 certified
  • Moulding sand reclaimed and reused
  • Steel scrap remelted in induction furnaces

Safe workplace

  • ISO 45001:2018 certified
  • Health and safety managed to a certified system
