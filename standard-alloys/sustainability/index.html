Responsible foundry

Steel that lasts longer is the first saving. We are also cutting the energy and emissions behind every tonne we pour.

A casting that lasts longer means fewer parts melted, shipped and replaced. That is where our work on grades and methods starts. In the plant, we have set science-based targets for our own emissions and put solar on our roofs.

  • 19.4%Scope 1 emissions reduction target, set to SBTi criteria
  • 11.2%Scope 2 emissions reduction target, set to SBTi criteria
  • 630 kWRooftop solar installed at the plant

Environmental management

  • ISO 14001:2015 certified
  • Moulding sand reclaimed and reused
  • Steel scrap remelted in induction furnaces

Safe workplace

  • ISO 45001:2018 certified
  • Health and safety managed to a certified system
