Who we are

Standard Alloys India began in 1991 as a fabrication shop in Ajmer and lit its first induction furnace in 1994. Today it is a specialist foundry for the parts that wear out first in cement, mining, aggregate, steel and power plants, and for the OEMs that build that equipment.

Up to seven tonnes in a single piece

About 14,000 tonnes of installed capacity a year. Single castings from 3 kg to 7,000 kg, with up to 10 tonnes of liquid metal in one pour.

Everything under one roof

Method simulation, pattern making, induction melting, no-bake moulding, heat treatment, machining to 6,500 mm and a full laboratory with NDT are all done in-house. Nothing that decides the life of a casting leaves our control.

Industries we serve

Parts for the equipment that runs around the clock, where an early failure stops a whole line.

Cement

Kiln and cooler castings, VRM rollers and table liners, mill liners and diaphragms.

Mining

Crusher liners, mill liners and wear parts for primary and secondary crushing.

Aggregate

Jaw plates, concaves, mantles, blow bars and hammers for quarries.

Steel and power

Heat-resistant castings for furnaces and grinding parts for coal mills.

Equipment makers
