Who we are

Standard Alloys India began in 1991 as a fabrication shop in Rajasthan, India, and lit its first induction furnace in 1994. Today it is a specialist foundry for the parts that wear out first in cement, mining, aggregate, thermal power and steel plants, and for the equipment makers who build those machines.

Up to seven tonnes in a single piece

About 14,000 tonnes a year of installed capacity across three foundry facilities. Single castings run from 3 kg to 7,000 kg, with up to 10 tonnes of liquid metal in one pour.

In our own hands

Method design, pattern making, induction melting, no-bake moulding, heat treatment, machining and a full laboratory with NDT are all done in our own plant. What decides the life of a casting stays under our control.
